Planting

Over the past twenty-five years I have led two church plants myself, and have sent, supported or supervised several others.

G2 and The Belfrey

For fourteen years I was the ordained leader of G2, a church plant from The Belfrey (St Michael le Belfrey) in York, and Pioneer Minister at The Belfrey. G2 reached the millennial generation largely by apprenticing new leaders.

Alpha

I worked for fifteen years with Alpha International, first as a Regional Director and later as National Director. During that period I also led G2.

Alpha as a Church Planting Tool (Alpha International, 2017). A short and simple study of why the Alpha course and church planting fit together so naturally. It goes some way to explaining why Alpha has proved such a catalyst for planting.

Earlier work

I served as a pastor and church planter with Newfrontiers.

The St Hild Centre for Church Planting

At St Hild I founded the St Hild Centre for Church Planting.

Part of the college, the St Hild Centre for Church Planting is a national centre for church planting, working across denominations and streams. It trains and connects leaders at every level, from volunteer planters to senior strategic leaders. Its programmes run from lay training to a Doctor of Ministry, alongside conferences and consultancy for churches, planting teams and networks. Find out more at sthildplanting.org.

Intercultural churches

A growing part of my work concerns intercultural mission and racial justice. I am a member of the Diocese of York’s Intercultural Ministry Task Group, and more widely I am involved in a number of racial justice projects and in supporting intercultural churches.
