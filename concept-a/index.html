Christian Selvaratnam

Practical theologian, church planter, teacher and writer. York.

I am Dean of Church Planting at St Hild College and Adjunct Professor at Asbury Theological Seminary, where I am Director of Studies for the Doctor of Ministry in the UK and Europe. I am a priest in the Church of England and Associate Minister of St Stephen, Acomb. I worked for fifteen years with Alpha International, latterly as National Director, and for fourteen years I was the ordained leader of G2, a church plant from The Belfrey (St Michael le Belfrey) in York.
