About

I am The Revd Canon Dr Christian Selvaratnam. I read Mathematics and Theoretical Computation at the University of Warwick, and my first job was as a Church of England verger in Coventry. Since then I have managed a printing business, served as a pastor and church planter with Newfrontiers, worked for fifteen years with Alpha International, led G2 in York for fourteen years, and founded the St Hild Centre for Church Planting.

What I do now

I am Dean of Church Planting at St Hild College, Associate Minister of St Stephen, Acomb in York, and Director of Studies for the Asbury Theological Seminary Doctor of Ministry cohort in the UK and Europe.

As a practical theologian, my work sits at the intersection of scholarship, research and training, and most of it is done alongside practitioners. For thirty years my focus has been mission and discipleship, leadership development, church growth, church renewal and church planting — areas where the Church retains and rediscovers its authentic identity, and where it proclaims the gospel afresh to this generation.

I write from time to time for various publications, and am an occasional guest on podcasts and radio programmes. I am also an editor for two book series.

I am a Distinguished Canon to confirm: canon wording of Manchester Cathedral, a Visiting Fellow of St John’s College at Durham University, an Adjunct Professor at Asbury Theological Seminary, and an Evangelist to confirm: wording in the College of Archbishops’ Evangelists.

In addition to my teaching work, I serve on a number of boards related to mission, church renewal, church planting and education in the UK, Europe and North America.

Formation

I trained for ordained ministry in the Church of England at Cranmer Hall, joining the first cohort of Ordained Pioneer Ministers in 2008. I later completed my doctoral studies at Asbury Theological Seminary, exploring how the pedagogy of medieval English craft guilds might be applied to contemporary training for clergy. That research still shapes the programmes I design.

York

I live in York. With my wife I am a judge for several regional tourism awards and a mystery shopper. As time allows, I volunteer at my local Parkrun, and once a month I carve wooden spoons with a local group.

I also take photographs.

More recently I vibe-coded a Church of England Liturgical Calendar plugin for xbar.

Short biography (for introductions and programmes)

Christian Selvaratnam is Dean of Church Planting at St Hild College, where he founded the Centre for Church Planting, and Associate Minister of St Stephen, Acomb. A Church of England priest and practical theologian, he has spent thirty years in mission, leadership development and church planting, and was previously National Director of Alpha International. He has led two church plants and sent, supported or supervised several others. He teaches ordinands and doctoral candidates, and is a Visiting Fellow of St John’s College, Durham and Adjunct Professor at Asbury Theological Seminary. He lives in York.
