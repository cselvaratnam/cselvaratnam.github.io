Teaching

Most of my teaching is with St Hild College, from short courses for lay people through to doctoral programmes.

If you are a lay person exploring church planting

  • Seedbed: online training for lay people exploring vocations in church planting.
  • Seedbed: Starting microchurches: a training and coaching programme equipping lay leaders to start and lead microchurches.

If you lead a resource church or oversee plants

  • Camino: a teaching and coaching programme for resource church and oversight leaders.

If you are training for ordination or ministry

The Church of England and its main ecumenical partners train their ministers through Common Awards, a partnership with Durham University that validates the programmes taught in theological colleges across England, Wales and Scotland. I lead the Church Planting Track at St Hild, the route for students taking church planting as their area of focus, and I teach the Common Awards modules below.

  • Creating New Christian Communities (TMM1397), Level 4.
  • Introduction to Christian Discipleship (TMM1517), Level 4.
  • Aspects of Spirituality and Ministry (TMM2577), Level 5.
  • Church Planting in Perspective (TMM3861) and Advanced Church Planting in Perspective (TMM46120), delivered together at Levels 6 and 7.

If you are an experienced leader

I am the Director of Studies for the Asbury Theological Seminary Doctor of Ministry in the UK and Europe. It is a cohort-based doctorate for experienced leaders who stay in ministry while they study, bringing together academic work and their own field research. It is taught in a British and European setting, and aims to form practitioner-theologians.

Current cohorts

  • Church Planting in Evangelical, Sacramental and Pentecostal Perspective (2024–2027), under way.
  • Mission Entrepreneurship and Church Multiplication (2027–2030), recruiting now.
