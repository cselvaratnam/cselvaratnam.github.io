Research

My doctoral studies at Asbury Theological Seminary explored how the pedagogy of medieval English craft guilds might be applied to training clergy today. That research still shapes the programmes I design.

What came of it

  • The Craft of Church Planting: Exploring the Lost Wisdom of Apprenticeship (SCM Press, 2022), the publication of that research. The Craft of Church Planting on the books page.
  • ‘Is Church Planting a Craft? Training Lessons from Medieval Guilds’, in New Churches: A Theology (SCM Press, 2024). The chapter on the books page.
  • John Valentine gives The Craft of Church Planting dedicated treatment in Ministerial Formation for the Future of the Church (SCM Press, 2027). to confirm: forthcoming; check whether published Reviews and discussion on the books page.

In progress

  • From Every Nation: Becoming an Intercultural Church in a Multicultural Age, due 2027, based on research with fifty intercultural church practitioners. to confirm: publisher not stated
  • Exploring the Mixed Ecology of Church: How Churches Thrive Together (Grove Books, 2026). to confirm: no public listing found
  • Discipleship Revolution: The Wesleys and the Shape of a Methodist Movement, due 2027. to confirm: publisher not stated

Research and supervision interests

My areas of particular expertise are church planting, church growth and ministerial formation, and I have emerging interest in the use of artificial intelligence in education and ministry, and in racial justice and intercultural church. I am always glad to hear from prospective research students.

  • Church planting and church planting movements.
  • Mission theology, missiology and church growth.
  • Mixed-ecology models of church.
  • Leadership development, apprenticeship and ministerial formation.
  • Artificial intelligence in theological education and Christian ministry.
  • Racial justice and intercultural church.

Supervision

I supervise research students who are using theologically informed empirical research to identify the practices and conditions that foster church growth, church planting and church revitalisation, and help their leaders to flourish.

If you are thinking of research in one of these areas, write to me at christian [dot] selvaratnam [at] gmail [dot] com.
