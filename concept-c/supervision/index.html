Supervision and teaching

I supervise postgraduate research and teach at St Hild College, from short courses for lay people through to doctoral programmes.

Doctor of Ministry

Recruitment is now open for the next cohort, Mission Entrepreneurship and Church Multiplication, which runs from 2027 to 2030 – find out more and apply. It follows Church Planting in Evangelical, Sacramental and Pentecostal Perspective (2024–2027) and Church Planting in Post-Christendom Europe (2021–2024).

Cohorts: 2021–2024 · 2024–2027 · 2027–2030 (recruiting).

I am the Director of Studies for the Asbury Theological Seminary Doctor of Ministry in the UK and Europe. It is a cohort-based doctorate for experienced leaders who stay in ministry while they study, bringing together academic work and their own field research. It is taught in a British and European setting, and aims to form practitioner-theologians.

I supervise research students who are using theologically informed empirical research to identify the practices and conditions that foster church growth, church planting and church revitalisation, and help their leaders to flourish.

Research students

My areas of particular expertise are church planting, church growth and ministerial formation, and I have emerging interest in the use of artificial intelligence in education and ministry, and in racial justice and intercultural church. I am always glad to hear from prospective research students. The interests I supervise are:

  • Church planting and church planting movements.
  • Mission theology, missiology and church growth.
  • Mixed-ecology models of church.
  • Leadership development, apprenticeship and ministerial formation.
  • Artificial intelligence in theological education and Christian ministry.
  • Racial justice and intercultural church.

If you are thinking of research in one of these areas, write to me at christian [dot] selvaratnam [at] gmail [dot] com.

Common Awards modules

The Church of England and its main ecumenical partners train their ministers through Common Awards, a partnership with Durham University that validates the programmes taught in theological colleges across England, Wales and Scotland. I lead the Church Planting Track at St Hild, the route for students taking church planting as their area of focus, and I teach the Common Awards modules below.

Validated by Durham University.
  • Creating New Christian Communities (TMM1397), Level 4.
  • Introduction to Christian Discipleship (TMM1517), Level 4.
  • Aspects of Spirituality and Ministry (TMM2577), Level 5.
  • Church Planting in Perspective (TMM3861) and Advanced Church Planting in Perspective (TMM46120), delivered together at Levels 6 and 7.

Training for lay people and church leaders

  • Exploring a call: Seedbed, online training for lay people exploring vocations in church planting.
  • Starting a microchurch: Seedbed: Starting microchurches, a training and coaching programme equipping lay leaders to start and lead microchurches.
  • Leading a resource church or oversight: Camino, a teaching and coaching programme for resource church and oversight leaders.
  • A church exploring planting: Explore Church Planting, a five-part film series for churches exploring church planting.
