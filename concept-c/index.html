Christian Selvaratnam

I study and teach how new churches are planted and how their leaders are formed.

I am a practical theologian, Dean of Church Planting at St Hild College, and Adjunct Professor at Asbury Theological Seminary, where I am Director of Studies for the Doctor of Ministry in the UK and Europe. My book The Craft of Church Planting (SCM Press, 2022) came from my doctoral research on how the pedagogy of medieval English craft guilds might be applied to training clergy today. I worked for fifteen years with Alpha International, latterly as National Director, and for fourteen years I was the ordained leader of G2, a church plant from The Belfrey (St Michael le Belfrey) in York. I am a priest in the Church of England and Associate Minister of St Stephen, Acomb, in York, where I live.

Three areas of work

IApprenticeship and formation

How the training methods of the medieval craft guilds might apply to the training of church planters and clergy.

Lead: The Craft of Church Planting (2022)

IIWhy and how churches are planted

The theological and practical reasons for planting, and the tools that help.

Lead: Why Plant Churches? (2023)

IIIThe mixed ecology and intercultural church

How different kinds of church live alongside one another, and how churches become intercultural.

Lead: ‘How churches can be intercultural’, Church Times (2025).
