Talks and articles

Not everything finds its way into a book. This page gathers some of the rest, by kind and newest first: articles, broadcasts and podcasts, talks and sermons, and blog posts.

If you would like me to speak at a conference or training day, please tell me the date, the place, who will be there and roughly how many, and what you would like me to speak on. I am also glad to talk about research. Write to me at christian [dot] selvaratnam [at] gmail [dot] com.

For training and consultancy for your church, planting team or network, see the St Hild Centre for Church Planting.
