Research

My doctoral studies at Asbury Theological Seminary explored how the pedagogy of medieval English craft guilds might be applied to contemporary training for clergy. What I have published, taught and said since falls into three areas, set out below with what is in progress.

IApprenticeship and formation

That research still shapes the programmes I design.

  • The Craft of Church Planting (2022)
    Reviewed by John Inge (Church Times), Helen Wordsworth (Baptist Times) and Robert Innes (Theology).
  • ‘Is Church Planting a Craft? Training Lessons from Medieval Guilds’ (2024).
  • John Valentine, Ministerial Formation for the Future of the Church (2027), which gives The Craft of Church Planting dedicated treatment.
    to confirm: forthcoming; check whether published
  • ‘Church Planting Apprenticeship: Ancient Wisdom’, Pivot (2025).
  • ‘How to Train Leaders’ and ‘How to Recruit Leaders’, LED24 (2024).
  • ‘Is Christian ministry artistry?’, Network (2024).
  • Teaching: the Asbury Doctor of Ministry.
  • Research interest: Leadership development, apprenticeship and ministerial formation.

IIWhy and how churches are planted

Church planting is often reduced to a task of mission. These are my writings and teaching on the wider theological and practical reasons, and the tools.

  • Why Plant Churches? (2023)
    Interviewed about it by Ian Paul for Psephizo, January 2024.
  • Alpha as a Church Planting Tool (2017).
  • ‘Reclaiming the Missing Generation’ (2019).
    A case study of G2.
  • Church Planting Bibliography (2026).
    Nearly 80 reading lists; preliminary edition, September 2026.
  • ‘Church-Planting, the State of the Church in Europe, and Alpha’, Art of Holiness (2025).
  • Teaching: Church Planting in Perspective (TMM3861) and Advanced Church Planting in Perspective (TMM46120), on Common Awards modules.
  • Research interest: Church planting and church planting movements.

IIIThe mixed ecology and intercultural church

A growing part of my work concerns intercultural mission and racial justice.

  • ‘Exploring Social Dynamics within the Mixed Ecology of Church’, The Bede Centre (2024).
  • ‘Resources churches: the Marmite effect’ and ‘Resource churches: To sow, plant, or graft?’, Church Times (2024).
    Quoted. to confirm: headline spelling as published
  • ‘How churches can be intercultural’, Church Times (2025).
  • ‘The Nations on Campus: Sending, Receiving and Reaching’ (2026).
  • Member of the Diocese of York Intercultural Ministry Task Group.
  • Teaching: Camino, for resource church and oversight leaders.
  • Research interests: Mixed-ecology models of church. Racial justice and intercultural church.
