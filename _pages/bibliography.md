**Church Planting Bibliography: Annotated Reading Lists for Students** (St Hild Centre for Church Planting, 2026).

This page is for those who teach church planting. The book began as a supplementary reading list for my own students and has grown into a reference book for the subject. I offer it to you, one teacher to another, as a resource to help you set up a module or strengthen one you already teach.

## What it contains

Nearly 80 reading lists I have compiled, covering more than 800 annotated books and resources. The lists fall into two sections: the core subjects of church planting, and wider reading on adjacent subjects. Each list opens with a short introduction, and each entry notes what the resource is and where it is most useful. A glossary explains more than 100 terms students meet in the literature, short biographies introduce more than 40 key authors, and an index of authors completes the book.

The selection reflects my own teaching. It is weighted towards England, where I mostly teach, and towards Protestant and evangelical writing, which makes up most of the literature. The book's introduction sets out these limitations in more detail.

## About the author

**Christian Selvaratnam** is Dean of Church Planting at St Hild College, where he founded the Centre for Church Planting, and Associate Minister of St Stephen, Acomb. A Church of England priest and practical theologian, he has spent thirty years in mission, leadership development and church planting, and was previously National Director of Alpha International. He has led two church plants and sent, supported or supervised several others. He teaches ordinands and doctoral candidates, and is a Visiting Fellow of St John's College, Durham and Adjunct Professor at Asbury Theological Seminary. He lives in York.

**St Hild** is a theological college serving Yorkshire and the wider region. It holds together academic theology and mission-focused Christian practice, and offers undergraduate and postgraduate awards through Durham University. St Hild trains people for lay and ordained roles in the Church of England, the Baptist Union and other traditions, provides continuing education for those already in ministry, and runs a dedicated track of church planting modules with specialist tutoring. Find out more at [sthild.org](https://sthild.org).

Part of the college, the **St Hild Centre for Church Planting** is a national centre for church planting, working across denominations and streams. It trains and connects leaders at every level, from volunteer planters to senior strategic leaders. Its programmes run from lay training to a Doctor of Ministry, alongside conferences and consultancy for churches, planting teams and networks. Find out more at [sthildplanting.org](https://sthildplanting.org).
