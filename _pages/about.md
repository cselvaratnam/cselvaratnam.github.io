I am Dean of Church Planting at [St Hild College](https://www.sthild.org), Associate Minister of [St Stephen, Acomb](https://www.acombparish.org) in York, and Director of Studies for the [Asbury Theological Seminary](https://asburyseminary.edu) Doctor of Ministry cohort in the UK.

I am a practitioner theologian. My work sits at the intersection of scholarship, research and training, and nearly all of it is done alongside practitioners — the people actually starting and leading churches.

## St Hild College

I founded the [St Hild Centre for Church Planting](https://www.sthildplanting.org), and I oversee the [Church Planting Track](https://www.sthild.org/church-planting-track) of Common Awards modules, [Seedbed](https://www.sthildplanting.org/seedbed) (a lay training pathway) and [Camino](https://www.sthildplanting.org/camino) (a programme for resource church leaders). I teach ordinands, postgraduate students and experienced clergy, and I supervise postgraduate research.

## Intercultural mission and racial justice

I am Intercultural Advisor in the Diocese of York, part of the diocese's Intercultural Ministry Task Group, having previously served as a Racial Justice Advocate in the diocese and as a founding member of the York Racial Justice Task Force.

I am Principal Investigator for *Intercultural North* (2026–27), the research strand of the Faith in the North intercultural mission pilot, conducting fieldwork with intercultural and ethnolinguistic churches across the northern dioceses. I am also writing a book on becoming an intercultural church.

## Writing and media

I write three times a year for the *Church Times*, and occasionally for local publications. Over two decades I have contributed to media and broadcasting, primarily through audio — radio, long-form interviews and podcasts — most recently presenting for *Beyond Belief* on BBC Radio 4.

I edit two book series: Durham Studies in Missiology and Ecclesiology, published by SCM Press, and the Grove Multiplication series, which I founded and convene.

My books are on the [books page](/books/), and shorter pieces are on the [writing page](/writing/).

## Before this

I worked for fifteen years with [Alpha International](https://www.alpha.org), including as National Director. During that period I also served for fourteen years as the ordained leader of G2, a church plant from [The Belfrey (St Michael le Belfrey)](https://www.belfrey.org) in York, and as Pioneer Minister at The Belfrey.

I read Mathematics and Theoretical Computation at the University of Warwick. My first job in the Church of England was as a verger in Coventry. I went on to manage a printing business and to serve as a pastor and church planter with Newfrontiers, before training for ordained ministry at [Cranmer Hall](https://www.cranmerhall.com) and joining the first cohort of Ordained Pioneer Ministers in 2008.

I completed my doctoral studies at Asbury Theological Seminary, exploring how the pedagogy of medieval English craft guilds might be applied to contemporary training for clergy. That research still shapes the programmes I design.

Over the past twenty years I have led two church plants myself, and have sent, supported or supervised several others. I now serve on a number of boards related to mission, church renewal, church planting and education in the UK, Europe and North America.

I am a Distinguished Canon of [Manchester Cathedral](https://manchestercathedral.org), a Visiting Fellow of [St John's College, Durham University](https://www.durham.ac.uk/colleges-and-student-experience/colleges/st-johns/), an Adjunct Professor at [Asbury Theological Seminary](https://asburyseminary.edu), and a member of the [General Synod](https://www.churchofengland.org/about/governance/general-synod) of the Church of England.

## Personal

I live in York. With my wife I judge several regional tourism awards and work as a mystery shopper for a number of organisations, reviewing visitor attractions, restaurants and hotels. I volunteer at my local parkrun as time allows, and once a month I carve wooden spoons with a local group.
