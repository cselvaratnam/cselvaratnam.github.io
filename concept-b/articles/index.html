Articles, talks and broadcasts

Not everything finds its way into a book. This page gathers some of the rest: articles and blog posts, podcasts, reviews, recordings of talks, and broadcasts. My books are on the books page, and my wider teaching on the study page.

2026

  • ‘Three Countries and Three Ways to Pay for Lunch’ Substack, 28 August 2026. Tipping culture and eating out in America, England and France in a single month. (blog)
  • ‘Learning from the American Civil Rights Story’ Substack, 3 August 2026. Two days in Birmingham and Atlanta in the American south, and what they taught me about the civil rights movement. (blog)
  • Church Going, with Giles Fraser, BBC Radio 4, July 2026. I contributed to three episodes of Giles Fraser’s series on the state of Christianity in England: episode 1, episode 2 and episode 3. (radio)
  • ‘The Nations on Campus: Sending, Receiving and Reaching’ Fusion USA Learning Community, 28 May 2026. A talk on mission to the nations for campus ministry now that the nations have come to us. (talk)

2025

  • ‘How churches can be intercultural’ Church Times, 5 December 2025. Becoming an intercultural parish church is more than welcoming people of different backgrounds. (article)
  • ‘Miracles that point to Jesus’ Manchester Cathedral, 5 October 2025. The sermon at the installation of the honorary canons, on the healing of the man born blind in John 9. Reported by the Diocese of Manchester. (sermon)
  • ‘Non-Anxious Leadership from the Coventry Blitz’ Substack, 5 May 2025. (blog)
  • Facebook, 23 April 2025. A note on Bible Society’s The Quiet Revival, written days after the report appeared. I argued that its headline claims of a sharp rise in church attendance among young adults were not plausible against what I was seeing on the ground, and that the interpretation was as much of a problem as the data. Bible Society withdrew the report in March 2026, after YouGov found weaknesses in the survey sample. (short post)
  • ‘Church-Planting, the State of the Church in Europe, and Alpha’ Art of Holiness with Carolyn Moore, 21 April 2025. (podcast)
  • ‘Church Planting Apprenticeship: Ancient Wisdom’ Pivot, Faith+Lead, 13 March 2025. On church planting, leading from the middle and fostering a mixed ecology of new and legacy churches. Also on video. (podcast)
  • ‘A Reflection on Lent’ St Hild College, 28 February 2025. (article) to confirm: no longer online
  • ‘New Wine Celebration’ St George’s Church, 24 February 2025. (video)
  • ‘Apostolic Leadership’ LederOase, 5 February 2025. A talk given in Denmark, in English. (audio)

2024

  • Development Day, Diocese of Sheffield, October 2024. The keynote address, and a workshop on developing speakers and preachers. (video)
  • ‘Michaelmas ordinations: Putting on the full armour of God’ Church Times, 4 October 2024. Why understanding trauma and building resilience now belong in training for ordained ministry. (article)
  • ‘Is Christian ministry artistry?’ Network, Diocese of Sheffield, autumn 2024. (article)
  • ‘Resources churches: the Marmite effect’ to confirm: headline spelling Church Times, 9 August 2024. Madeleine Davies’s two-part investigation into resource churches, for which I was interviewed and quoted. Part two: ‘Resource churches: To sow, plant, or graft?’, 16 August 2024. (quoted)
  • ‘C of E should try this recipe for growth’ Church Times, 7 June 2024. There is a better way to reverse falling attendance than merging struggling parishes. (article)
  • ‘Exploring Social Dynamics within the Mixed Ecology of Church’ The Bede Centre for Church Planting Theology, 27 March 2024. (video)
  • ‘Resilience in Ministry’ a keynote at the New Wine Leadership Conference, Harrogate, February 2024. Watching it requires a New Wine Online subscription. (video)
  • LED24, Pinsebevegelsen i Norge, February 2024. Two talks at a leadership conference in Norway: ‘How to Train Leaders’ and ‘How to Recruit Leaders’. (video)
  • ‘Why plant churches?’ Psephizo, 24 January 2024. Ian Paul interviews me about the Grove booklet. (interview)
