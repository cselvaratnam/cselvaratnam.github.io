Christian Selvaratnam

I have led two church plants, and I train the people who plant churches, from lay people starting out to experienced leaders on a doctorate.

I am Dean of Church Planting at St Hild College, where I founded the St Hild Centre for Church Planting, and Adjunct Professor at Asbury Theological Seminary, where I am Director of Studies for the Doctor of Ministry in the UK and Europe. I worked for fifteen years with Alpha International, latterly as National Director, and for fourteen years I was the ordained leader of G2, a church plant from The Belfrey (St Michael le Belfrey) in York. I am a priest in the Church of England and Associate Minister of St Stephen, Acomb.
