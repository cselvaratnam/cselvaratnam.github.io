About

I am The Revd Canon Dr Christian Selvaratnam. My first job was as a Church of England verger in Coventry; I then managed a printing business and served as a pastor and church planter with Newfrontiers.

I worked for fifteen years with Alpha International, first as a Regional Director and later as National Director. During that period I also served for fourteen years as the ordained leader of G2, a church plant from The Belfrey (St Michael le Belfrey) in York, and as Pioneer Minister at The Belfrey.

I trained for ordained ministry in the Church of England at Cranmer Hall, joining the first cohort of Ordained Pioneer Ministers in 2008. I later completed my doctoral studies at Asbury Theological Seminary, exploring how the pedagogy of medieval English craft guilds might be applied to contemporary training for clergy. That research still shapes the programmes I design.

Over the past twenty-five years I have led two church plants myself, and have sent, supported or supervised several others. In addition to my teaching work, I serve on a number of boards related to mission, church renewal, church planting and education in the UK, Europe and North America.

What I do now

I am Dean of Church Planting at St Hild College, Associate Minister of St Stephen, Acomb in York, and Director of Studies for the Asbury Theological Seminary Doctor of Ministry cohort in the UK and Europe.

At St Hild I founded the St Hild Centre for Church Planting, and I oversee the St Hild Church Planting Track of Common Awards modules, Seedbed (a lay training pathway), Camino (a programme for resource church leaders) and a Doctor of Ministry programme with Asbury Seminary. I teach Anglican ordinands and Baptist ministers-in-training, experienced clergy and postgraduate students, and I supervise postgraduate research.

A growing part of my work concerns intercultural mission and racial justice. I am a member of the Diocese of York’s Intercultural Ministry Task Group, and more widely I am involved in a number of racial justice projects and in supporting intercultural churches. I also have an emerging interest in the use of artificial intelligence in education, research and ministry.

I write from time to time for various publications, and am an occasional guest on podcasts and radio programmes. I am also an editor for two book series.

I am a Distinguished Canon to confirm: canon wording of Manchester Cathedral, a Visiting Fellow of St John’s College at Durham University, an Adjunct Professor at Asbury Theological Seminary, and an Evangelist to confirm: wording in the College of Archbishops’ Evangelists.

York

I read Mathematics and Theoretical Computation at the University of Warwick.

I live in York. With my wife I am a judge for several regional tourism awards and a mystery shopper. As time allows, I volunteer at my local Parkrun, and once a month I carve wooden spoons with a local group.

I also take photographs.
