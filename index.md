I'm **Christian Selvaratnam**, Dean of Church Planting at [St Hild College](https://www.sthild.org/) and Adjunct Professor at [Asbury Theological Seminary](https://asburyseminary.edu), a priest in the [Church of England](https://www.churchofengland.org) and Associate Minister of [St Stephen](https://www.acombparish.org/), Acomb in York. I am a practical theologian, a church planter and an educator, training people theologically and practically for ministry and senior roles in the Church. I live in York.

This is a small site, somewhere to keep my work in one place: the [books and chapters](/books/) I have written, my wider [teaching](/teaching/), and [everything else](/misc/).

I am always glad to hear from people about church planting, research and supervision, or speaking opportunities. Contact me at christian [dot] selvaratnam [at] gmail [dot] com.
