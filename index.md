Hello, and welcome.

I teach church planting at St Hild College in Yorkshire. Most of my working
life has been spent starting new churches and helping other people start
theirs, and rather a lot of it thinking about how they are trained for it.

This is a small site. It is really just somewhere to keep my work in one
place: the [books and chapters](/books/) I have written, [shorter
pieces](/writing/) for the *Church Times* and elsewhere, and the
[courses](/teaching/) I teach. If you need the formal version, there is a
[biography](/about/).

Thank you for stopping by.
