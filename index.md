Hello, and welcome — thank you for stopping by.

This is a small site, somewhere to keep my work in one place: the [books and chapters](/books/) I have written, [shorter pieces](/writing/), the [courses](/teaching/) I teach, and my [biography](/about/).

I teach at St Hild College in Yorkshire. Most of my working life has been spent starting new churches and helping other people start theirs, thinking with them about the theological and practical questions around leadership development, discipleship, mission, church renewal and church planting.
