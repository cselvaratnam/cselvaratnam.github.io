Hello, and welcome — thank you for stopping by.

I'm **Christian Selvaratnam**, Dean of Church Planting at [St Hild College](https://www.sthild.org/) and Associate Minister of [St Stephen](https://www.acombparish.org/) Acomb in York (more [here](/about/)). I am a practical theologian, a church planter and an educator, training people theologically and practically for ministry in the church and senior roles. I live in York.

This is a small site, somewhere to keep my work in one place: the [books and chapters](/books/) I have written, [shorter pieces](/writing/), the [courses](/courses/) I teach, and my [biography](/about/).
